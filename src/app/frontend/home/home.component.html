About Us
Our love towards coir commenced on 1974 as Kali coir industries which is one of the oldest and promising manufacturing unit started by Late Thiru K.Kuppusamy gounder in agri land pollachi .

And with relentless efforts on quality based production our 2nd manufacturing unit Jai Sri coir products was commenced on 2010 by P.K.N Kalanidhi Babu. And passing through 4 different generations, with 47 years of experience in coir industry makes COIR HUB prominent and successful.
Vision
Intend to bring reforms in the farming practices and in the environment through our quality coco products to lead a quality life.
Mission
To manufacture a range of biodegradable coco products and to expand the business beyond boundaries with the main concern on environment and customer satisfaction.
